About Flora Complex

Flora Complex consists of four interconnected buildings — Flora Complex, Tulip Residence, Clover Residence and Violet Residence. Located in the heart of Borovets, just 250m from the ski gondola, it offers some of the most sought-after addresses in the resort. 24/7 reception, underground parking and direct walking access to everything the resort has to offer.
